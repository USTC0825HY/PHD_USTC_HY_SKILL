# MAROC PPT Group Meeting

## Structure

1. One-slide agenda with page ranges.
2. Why this stage matters in the MAROC bring-up chain.
3. Recent work and tested objects.
4. Key result slides with large screenshots/plots.
5. One slide per open problem or decision request.
6. Next week plan and required lab resources.

## Rules

Prefer readable projector pages over dense debug logs. Use `$maroc-ppt` for style and `maroc-evidence-ppt-sync` for evidence wording.
